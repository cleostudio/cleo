Some days you want prose. Some days you want to wander. [Gallery](/gallery) is
for the second mood: the same curated frames as the field guides, arranged so
photographs lead.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x719 "Geirangerfjord, Norway")

![](./image-2.jpg#1280x1280 "The Orion Nebula")

## One wall

Gallery unifies two catalogs without becoming two apps:

- Place photographs from Explore
- Body photographs from Space

Filters narrow by origin; they do not split the product. A fjord can sit beside
a nebula because both frames already belong to guides on this site.

## Why separate

Guides keep a reading rhythm — orientation first, then prints. Gallery flips
that order. Here the photograph is the headline; caption and subject link name
what you see and where to read more.

If every guide opened as a masonry wall, orientation would drown. If Gallery
forced an essay above the fold, wandering would feel like homework. Choose the
door: Topics for the map, Explore or Space for a primer, Gallery when you want
to look first.
