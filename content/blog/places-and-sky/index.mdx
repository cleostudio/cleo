Some days you want prose. Some days you want to wander through photographs.
[Gallery](/gallery) is for the second mood.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x853 "A place photograph from the Explore atlas")

![](./image-2.jpg#1280x1280 "A Space photograph from the NASA catalog")

## One wall

Gallery unifies two catalogs:

- Place photos from country Explore guides
- Body photos from Space guides

Filters let you narrow by origin without splitting the site into two photo
apps. `/photos` permanently redirects here so old links still land on the same
wall.

## Why separate

Guides keep a reading rhythm — orientation first, then prints. Gallery flips
that: images lead, and each frame can send you back to its subject. The two
surfaces share assets; they do not share jobs.
