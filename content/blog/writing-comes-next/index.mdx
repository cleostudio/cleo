Field guides answer what a place or body is. Writing answers the questions
around them — how this portal thinks, and subjects that need an essay instead of
a passport page.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x853 "A lane in the Medina of Fez, Morocco")

## Two kinds

[Writing](/blog) holds two kinds of essay:

- **Portal** — how Explore, Space, Gallery, Topics, and the agent fit together
- **Subject** — durable readings grounded in the catalogs, such as
  [Japan and Iceland](/blog/japan-and-iceland),
  [Europa and Enceladus](/blog/icy-moons-europa-enceladus), and
  [Reading Mars](/blog/reading-mars)

Portal essays explain the shelves. Subject essays start to fill them. Neither
replaces a field guide, and neither replaces [Cleo](/cleo). Conversation stays
in the agent; orientation prose stays curated on Explore and Space.

## Shape

Expect short titles, short section headers, and links back into real routes.
A post should usually do one job. Covers and in-post images are local files
beside the MDX. If a sentence does not help you understand a subject or the
portal, it does not belong here.

## Ahead

As [Topics](/topics) expands, Writing can hold comparisons and longer primers
that do not fit a field-guide template — still English-only, still reviewed
before they ship. Start with [Welcome to Cleo](/blog/welcome-to-cleo), then
wander into a subject piece. The next click after an essay should be concrete:
a country, a body, a wall of photographs, or a question for the agent.
