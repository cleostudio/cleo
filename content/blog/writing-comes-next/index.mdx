Field guides answer “what is this place or body?” Writing answers “how does
this portal think?” and, over time, longer subjects that deserve an essay
instead of a passport page.

We are not rushing that second job into a fake encyclopedia overnight. The
honest sequence is smaller: ship Explore and Space as curated shelves, give
Gallery one wall, keep Topics short, and use Writing to narrate the editorial
decisions out loud. Those essays are already MDX in the repo — the same kind
of durable surface a deeper knowledge layer can grow into later.

## Role

[Writing](/blog) is MDX in the repo: titles, dates, and prose you can read
without a database. It is the same publishing path a future encyclopedia-like
layer can grow into — still English-only, still reviewed before it ships.

These early posts stay close to the product on purpose. They explain Explore,
Space, Gallery, Topics, and the agent so the rest of the site has a voice
besides UI chrome.

That voice is calm and collective. These pages talk about Cleo in the second
person and the first-person plural — what you can open, what we keep stable —
not a founder diary and not a changelog dressed up as memoir. Internal links
point at real routes: [/explore](/explore), [/space](/space),
[/gallery](/gallery), [/topics](/topics), [/blog](/blog), [/cleo](/cleo), and
specific guides when a name helps.

Writing does not generate country orientation at request time. That corpus
stays curated elsewhere. Writing also does not replace the agent. [Cleo](/cleo)
remains the place for conversation, web search, and images in the moment.
Essays are for durable explanation you can cite next month.

## Shape

Expect short titles, calm sections, and links back into real routes. You will
not find personal year-in-reviews here. You will find the editorial decisions
that keep Cleo feeling like one composition.

A post should usually do one job: welcome you to the portal, explain why
photos stay local, draw the line between curated guides and generated chat, or
name what Topics holds today. Section headers stay short — one or two words —
so the essay keeps a field-guide cadence even when the word count grows.

Covers and in-post images are local files beside the MDX. Frontmatter carries
title, description, date, and cover dimensions. There is no CMS round-trip and
no auth wall. If a sentence does not help you understand the portal, it
probably belongs in a different kind of site.

## Ahead

As Topics expands, Writing can hold the essays that do not fit a field-guide
template — comparisons, methods, and longer primers. Until then, start with
[Welcome to Cleo](/blog/welcome-to-cleo) and wander outward.

The long game is an encyclopedia-like layer that still feels like Cleo: curated
where it should be curated, conversational where conversation helps, and
honest about what exists today. Countries and Space are the first shelves.
Writing is the hallway where we explain the architecture before more doors
open.

When you finish an essay, the next click should be concrete — a country in
Explore, a body in Space, a wall in Gallery, or a question for the agent. The
words are here to send you back into the portal, not to keep you in a feed.
