Field guides answer what a place or body is. Writing answers the questions
around them — how this portal thinks, and subjects that need an essay instead of
a passport page.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x853 "A lane in the Medina of Fez, Morocco")

## Today

[Writing](/blog) is the essay shelf beside Explore and Space. These early posts
stay close to the product on purpose: they welcome you to the portal, explain
the field guides, defend curation, and show how the agent fits.

Read them as a short sequence:

1. [Welcome to Cleo](/blog/welcome-to-cleo) — the doorway
2. [Country field guides](/blog/country-field-guides) and
   [Space field guides](/blog/space-field-guides) — the two shelves
3. [Places and sky](/blog/places-and-sky) — when photographs lead
4. [Ask Cleo](/blog/ask-cleo) — conversation on the same catalogs
5. [Curated, not generated](/blog/curated-not-generated) and
   [Photos stay local](/blog/photos-stay-local) — the boundaries
6. [Topics first](/blog/topics-first) — how the catalog grows
7. [Japan and Iceland](/blog/japan-and-iceland),
   [Europa and Enceladus](/blog/icy-moons-europa-enceladus), and
   [Reading Mars](/blog/reading-mars) — subject essays

The tone is calm and collective. Internal links point at real routes. Portal
essays explain the shelves; subject essays start to fill them.

## Later

As [Topics](/topics) expands, Writing can hold comparisons, methods, and longer
primers that do not fit a field-guide template — still English-only, still
reviewed before they ship. Country orientation will not move into these essays;
that corpus stays curated on Explore. Conversation will not move here either;
that stays at [Cleo](/cleo).

The long game is an encyclopedia-like layer that still feels like Cleo: curated
where it should be curated, conversational where conversation helps, and honest
about what exists today. Countries and Space are the first shelves. Writing is
where portal essays and subject pieces share one reading surface.

## Out

When you finish an essay, the next click should be concrete — a country in
Explore, a body in Space, a wall in Gallery, or a question for the agent. The
words are here to send you back into the portal, not to keep you in a feed.
