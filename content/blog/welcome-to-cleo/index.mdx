Cleo is a place to learn about the world and the sky without turning either into
a feed. Search a country, open a planet, wander photographs, then ask a
follow-up — all on one small English-only site.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x1280 "Earth — the Blue Marble, from the Space catalog")

## Portal

The homepage is a doorway. From there the map stays short:

- [Explore](/explore) for country field guides
- [Space](/space) for the Solar System, moons, and deep space
- [Gallery](/gallery) when photographs should lead
- [Topics](/topics) for the subject list as it grows
- [Writing](/blog) for essays about how the portal thinks
- [Cleo](/cleo) for conversation on the same catalogs

Each route keeps one job. Explore and Space orient. Gallery lets images lead.
Topics names the doors. Writing explains the architecture. The agent handles
questions a static page should not chase.

## Tempo

Field guides are meant to stay put — curated orientation you can bookmark.
Conversation is where freshness belongs. Photographs are local files, not a
remote media account. Chat lives in your browser and clears on reload.

That split is the product. The rest of Writing spells out the pieces; this page
is only the welcome.

## Start

Open [Explore](/explore) for a place, [Space](/space) for a body, or
[Cleo](/cleo) when you want to talk it through. A good first hour: read one
country guide, zoom a contact print, drift through Gallery until a Space frame
catches you, then ask the agent to compare what you just saw.
