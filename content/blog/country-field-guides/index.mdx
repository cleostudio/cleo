An Explore page is a field notebook for one country: enough orientation to stand
somewhere, then photographs of places that actually belong there.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x853 "Maasai Mara, Kenya")

## Format

Open any country at `/explore/[slug]`. Warm paper, passport labels, and hairline
rules are intentional — a guide in hand, not a CMS article.

You get a short orientation (place, climate, character), facts chosen to age
slowly, and a contact-print sequence you can zoom. Orientation is curated once:
no recycled sentences across countries, no claims that spoil in weeks. Why that
line exists is [Curated, not generated](/blog/curated-not-generated).

## Places

Photographs are chosen for relevance first, then published with the guide.
[Gallery](/gallery) reuses the same frames when you want to look without opening
a primer.

Try [Japan](/explore/japan), [Iceland](/explore/iceland), or
[Kenya](/explore/kenya) — or search from the [homepage](/). Space is the other
first shelf, at [Space](/space). For a country comparison in Writing, see
[Japan and Iceland](/blog/japan-and-iceland).
