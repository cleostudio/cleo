A knowledge portal feels thin the moment every page reads like the same model
filled a template. Cleo draws a hard line: guides are curated; conversation is
generated.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x805 "The Colosseum at dusk, Rome")

## Line

Country orientation lives in a maintained prose corpus. Drafts are checked for
length, recycled phrasing, and claims that age in weeks. No sentence may appear
in two countries. Opening an Explore page never calls a model to invent the
prose.

Space primers follow the same instinct — stable orientation and features, not a
regenerated summary on each visit. Photographs are chosen and imported before
publish.

Generation still has a home at [Cleo](/cleo): search, reason, reply, forget the
transcript on reload. What we refuse is a page that secretly regenerates itself
when you arrive.

## Why

A bookmark should still be trustworthy next month. Editors should be able to
review every claim. The agent stays useful because the rest of the site is not
improvising underneath it.

Pointing at [Iceland](/explore/iceland) or [Mars](/space/mars) only helps when
those pages are durable destinations.

## Agent

[Cleo](/cleo) is the right place for freshness — comparisons, vision questions,
sketches, today’s evidence. When the durable answer already exists as a field
guide, the agent links to it instead of replacing it.

Curated, not generated, is not a slogan against AI. It is a boundary that keeps
two tempos honest: pages you can trust, and a conversation you can clear.
