Most knowledge sites grow by promising everything. Cleo grows by naming what you
can open today.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x720 "The Giza pyramid complex, Egypt")

## Two doors

[Topics](/topics) currently lists:

- **Countries** — into [Explore](/explore) field guides and place photographs
- **Space** — into [Space](/space) primers for planets, moons, and deep space

Those doors also appear on the homepage. [Gallery](/gallery) sits beside both
when photographs should lead. The agent at [Cleo](/cleo) reads the same map: if
a subject is listed, a real route should exist behind it.

Two doors are enough for a useful hour — [Egypt](/explore/egypt) or
[Mars](/space/mars) — without pretending every subject already has a shelf.

## The bar

A third topic ships when it can stand beside the first two: curated orientation,
honest photographs, and paths the agent can deep-link without guessing. Until
then the list stays short. “More later” is a constraint, not a tease.

[Writing](/blog) already mixes portal essays with subject pieces so the shelves
have a voice besides UI chrome. Topics names the doors; essays and guides do
the reading.

## Browse

Open [Topics](/topics) for the map. From Countries, try
[Iceland](/explore/iceland) or [Kenya](/explore/kenya). From Space, start with
[Saturn](/space/saturn) or the [ISS](/space/iss). When looking should lead,
switch to Gallery. When a question wants a voice, ask Cleo.
