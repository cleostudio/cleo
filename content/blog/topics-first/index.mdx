Cleo means to grow as a general-knowledge portal. Growth starts with a short
list, not a promise of everything.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x851 "Egypt, from the Explore atlas")

## Now

[Topics](/topics) currently names two doors:

- **Countries** — Explore field guides and place photographs
- **Space** — Solar System, moons, and deep-space primers

Those doors also appear on the homepage beside country search and highlighted
places. Countries lead into [Explore](/explore). Space leads into
[Space](/space). [Gallery](/gallery) sits beside both as one filterable wall.

The agent at [Cleo](/cleo) reads the same map. When a question matches a guide,
the reply should deep-link a real path. Topics makes that promise legible: if
it is on the list, the routes behind it exist.

## Later

More topics arrive when they meet the same bar — curated orientation, honest
media, and routes the agent can name without guessing. Until then the catalog
stays short on purpose.

Former `/projects` URLs redirect here so old bookmarks still find a subject
index. “More later” is a constraint, not a tease: a new topic should bring
guides you can review and photographs that ship as files, not a second product
stack.

[Writing](/blog) narrates those decisions while the shelves grow. Topics names
the doors; essays explain the architecture; Explore and Space do the reading.

## Browse

Open [Topics](/topics) for the map. Open a topic for the shelves inside it.

From Countries, try [Iceland](/explore/iceland) or [Kenya](/explore/kenya). From
Space, start with [Mars](/space/mars) or the [ISS](/space/iss). When photographs
should lead, switch to Gallery. When a question wants a voice, ask Cleo.

Two doors are enough to begin. The next one opens when it deserves a key.
