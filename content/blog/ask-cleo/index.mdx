[Cleo](/cleo) is the AI agent on this portal — not a separate product bolted
beside it. It knows the same catalogs the rest of the site publishes, so a
question about a country or a moon can send you back to a real field guide.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x853 "Enceladus, from the Space catalog")

## Skills

In one conversation you can:

- Ask in plain language
- Search the live web when a fact needs fresh evidence
- Attach an image for vision
- Generate a picture when a sketch helps
- Receive Markdown links into Explore and Space
- See a curated atlas or space photograph when the topic matches

Chat streams into the page; nothing is stored server-side. State lives in your
browser and clears on reload. There is no login. Stop a reply whenever you have
seen enough.

## Fit

Field guides stay curated and static. The agent is the flexible layer: compare
two moons, notice what a landscape shows, or chase a follow-up the primer
leaves open.

When a subject has a page here, Cleo should name it and link the real path —
[Japan](/explore/japan), [Europa](/space/europa) — instead of inventing a URL.
Matching turns may embed a curated site photograph. Arbitrary remote images do
not get laundered into the transcript.

Bookmark an Explore guide and it still says the same thing next month. Ask about
today’s news and the agent can search. Ask about a frame you attached and it can
look. The catalogs do not regenerate underneath you — that boundary is
[Curated, not generated](/blog/curated-not-generated).

## Open

From the bottom dock, open Cleo (`G` then `C`), or go to [/cleo](/cleo). Starter
prompts on the empty state point into the portal; after that, just ask.

A concrete loop: read [Mars](/space/mars) or [Japan](/explore/japan) until a
question forms, then bring it here. When the durable answer already lives in a
guide, you should get a link home. When it does not, you get a conversation —
and a clean slate on reload.
