Space on Cleo is not a mission ticker. It is a shelf of evergreen primers —
bodies you can return to — from Mercury to nearby deep space.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x946 "Europa, from the Space catalog")

## Shelves

The catalog opens in three groups:

- Solar System — planets and the Sun
- Moons — major satellites, plus the [ISS](/space/iss) as a human outpost
- Deep Space — nearby galaxies and nebulae

Every subject lives at `/space/[slug]` with orientation, notable features, and a
short fact plate. The goal matches Explore: leave you oriented, not flooded.

[Topics](/topics) lists Space beside Countries as one of the two doors that open
the portal today. The full shelf is at [Space](/space).

## Photos

Space photographs come from curated NASA sources and ship as static files under
`/images/space/`. Like the country atlas, they are imported before publish and
served locally — no image account, no surprise rewrite when you open a page.
[Gallery](/gallery) hangs these frames on the same wall as place photographs.

Begin with [Mars](/space/mars), [Europa](/space/europa), or the
[ISS](/space/iss).

## Asking

Comparisons and tangents belong to [Cleo](/cleo). When a body has a guide, the
agent should name the real path and, on matching turns, show a curated
photograph. The field guide stays durable; conversation chases what the primer
leaves open.

If you are new to the shelf, read one planet, one moon, and one deep-space
subject slowly. Then filter Gallery to Space, or ask the agent to walk a
comparison. The sky here is small enough to finish — and rich enough to revisit.
