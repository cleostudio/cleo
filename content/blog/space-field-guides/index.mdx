Space on Cleo is not a mission ticker. It is a shelf of evergreen primers —
bodies you can return to — from the Sun to nearby deep space.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x853 "Andromeda, a deep-space guide subject")

## Shelves

The catalog opens in three groups:

- **Solar System** — the Sun, planets, and the [ISS](/space/iss)
- **Moons** — major natural satellites, including [Europa](/space/europa)
- **Deep Space** — nearby galaxies and nebulae such as the
  [Orion Nebula](/space/orion-nebula) and [Andromeda](/space/andromeda)

Every subject lives at `/space/[slug]` with orientation, notable features, and a
short fact plate. Leave oriented, not flooded.

## Looking

Space photographs come from curated NASA sources and ship with the guides.
[Gallery](/gallery) hangs them beside place photographs when you want the sky to
lead. How those files stay on the site is
[Photos stay local](/blog/photos-stay-local).

Begin with [Jupiter](/space/jupiter), [Mars](/space/mars), or
[Europa](/space/europa). For subject essays, try [Reading Mars](/blog/reading-mars)
or [Europa and Enceladus](/blog/icy-moons-europa-enceladus).

## Asking

Comparisons belong to [Cleo](/cleo). When a body has a guide, the agent should
name the real path. The field guide stays durable; conversation chases what the
primer leaves open.
