Photographs are half of a field guide. They also tend to become the most fragile
dependency on a modern site. Cleo keeps them boring on purpose.

We want a country page and a Space page to keep working in a preview, in a
local build, and on a quiet Sunday when no third-party image account feels
like answering. That means the bytes live in the repo’s public tree, the
browser chooses a width, and runtime stays out of the image business.

## Pipeline

Country photos are curated from Wikimedia Commons with relevance first, then
imported as optimized local JPEGs. Space photos follow a NASA curation pass
into the same kind of static tree.

Public files live under:

- `/images/atlas/{slug}/` for Explore places
- `/images/space/{slug}/` for Space bodies

Originals stay off the public tree. The browser picks a width from `srcset`.
There is no account-gated image host and no `/_next/image` rewrite at request
time.

Relevance beats decoration. A landmark should belong to the country on the
page; a Space frame should show the body you opened. Curation and hand-picks
happen before import. Import writes mozjpeg renditions at a few widths so a
phone and a wide monitor can each fetch something sensible. Validation scripts
catch missing files before deploy. The Gallery and the guides speak the same
paths, so a frame is never “in the CMS” in one place and “on a CDN” in
another.

Writing essays may include their own local images beside the post. That is a
separate folder under the MDX entry. The atlas and Space trees remain the
system of record for field-guide photography — the pictures [Explore](/explore)
and [Space](/space) zoom, and the pictures [Gallery](/gallery) unifies.

## Payoff

Pages keep working in previews without extra services. Guides stay reviewable
in git. The Gallery can unify both catalogs because both already speak the same
file language.

Boring infrastructure is a product choice. Without a runtime image pipeline,
we also skip a class of surprises: broken tokens, surprise re-encodes, and
layouts that shift when a remote host is slow. Diffs show when a photograph
changes. Contact sheets can be generated offline for review. The agent at
[Cleo](/cleo) can embed a matching curated path as Markdown because those
paths are already allowlisted site URLs — not scraped hotlinks.

OpenAI is the only third-party API this portal depends on, and it belongs to
conversation, not to every `<img>`. Site URLs and the model key stay in
environment config. Photographs do not need either one to render.

## Reading

Zoom a contact print on an Explore page, wander the [Gallery](/gallery), or ask
[Cleo](/cleo) about a subject that has a curated frame — the bytes are already
on the site.

Try it as a loop. Open [Japan](/explore/japan) or [Mars](/space/mars), zoom
until the print feels close, then switch to Gallery and filter by origin. When
a question forms — what am I seeing, what else is nearby, how does this compare
to [Europa](/space/europa) — take it to the agent. The photograph does not have
to travel through another vendor to finish that sentence. It was local the
whole time.
