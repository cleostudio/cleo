Photographs are half of a field guide. They are also where modern sites often
grow fragile. Cleo keeps them boring on purpose: the bytes live on the site,
and opening a page does not depend on a remote image account.

![](./image-1.jpg#1280x853 "A lane in the Medina of Fez, Morocco")

![](./image-2.jpg#1280x853 "The Carina Nebula")

## Choice

Country photographs are curated for relevance, then published with the Explore
guides. Space photographs follow a NASA curation pass into the same kind of
static library. [Gallery](/gallery) and the field guides speak one set of files.

Relevance beats decoration. A landmark should belong to the country on the
page; a Space frame should show the body you opened. The browser picks a width.
Runtime stays out of the image business.

## Payoff

Pages keep working in quiet previews. Guides stay reviewable as ordinary files.
The Gallery can unify places and sky because both catalogs already share a
format. When [Cleo](/cleo) embeds a matching photograph, it points at a site
path that was already allowlisted — not a scraped hotlink.

## Loop

Zoom a contact print on [Explore](/explore), wander [Gallery](/gallery), or ask
[Cleo](/cleo) about a subject that has a curated frame. Try [Peru](/explore/peru)
or the [Carina Nebula](/space/carina-nebula) — the photograph does not need
another vendor to finish the sentence.
